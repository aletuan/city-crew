## Description

### EN

```
City Crew is a curated guide to your city — not everything, just the places
worth your evening.

EXPLORE
A hand-picked catalog of cafés, restaurants, bars and spots, curated by an
editorial desk before anything reaches your screen. Open the app and it
opens on your city, instantly.

COLLECTIONS
Lists built by people who actually go: date nights, crew weekends, coffee
crawls. Follow what you like, or sign in and build your own.

PLANS
Tell it who you're with and what you feel like — get a quick, walkable plan
for tonight. Save it, tweak it, share it.

YOURS, PRIVATELY
Browsing needs no account. Sign in with just your email (one-time code, no
password) to save places and build lists. Your location never leaves your
phone — it's read once, on-device, to open on the nearest city. No ads, no
trackers. Delete your account in one tap, in the app.

Currently covering Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi, with more cities on the way.
```
